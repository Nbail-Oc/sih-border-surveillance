Architectural Specification and Research Report: 3-Tier Edge-to-Cloud AI Model Zoo for High-Precision Border SurveillanceOperational Context and Tactical BackgroundThe Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) and the Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB) operate under severe environmental, operational, and computational constraints across international border sectors under project IBVAP (Intelligent Border Video Analytics Platform / SIH PS-26187). Remote forward posts rely on low-power, passively cooled edge hardware constrained to 15–25 Watts, where processing latency must remain under 15 milliseconds per frame to facilitate real-time threat intervention. Camera feeds in these environments originate from standard visible-light IP surveillance infrastructure equipped with integrated 850nm or 940nm active infrared (IR) LED arrays rather than costly Long-Wave Infrared (LWIR) thermal sensors.Active infrared illumination presents unique computer vision challenges that differ fundamentally from thermal imagery. Active-IR feeds suffer from non-uniform illumination falloff, high-frequency sensor grain in distant low-light regions, severe highlight over-exposure (blooming) on reflective surfaces, and backscatter from atmospheric haze or dust. Furthermore, optical physical constraints mean human targets at distances exceeding 150 meters occupy fewer than 25 vertical pixels in standard 1080p feeds.To resolve these challenges, this architectural specification establishes a multi-modal 3-tier model zoo. Tier 1 represents the sole hardware profile implemented and benchmarked for hands-on prototype deployment. Technical Pipeline RationaleOn Tier 1 Edge CPU platforms, deep neural super-resolution architectures (such as Real-ESRGAN or SwinIR) are intentionally excluded due to their severe compute cost ($45\text{ ms} - 110\text{ ms}$ on CPU), which violates real-time latency budgets. Instead, IBVAP pairs classical OpenCV Contrast-Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (CLAHE) with PP-OCRv5-Mobile. Active-IR headlight glare often over-saturates retro-reflective aluminum HSRP surfaces, causing character boundaries to bleed into background noise. CLAHE operates on localized $8 \times 8$ tile contextual histograms in under 0.8ms on CPU, enhancing edge contrast around character borders prior to tokenization.PP-OCRv5-Mobile utilizes a data-centric architecture with 5 million parameters, delivering a 13 percentage point accuracy gain over PP-OCRv4 while processing recognition cycles in under 12ms on CPU. For Tier 2 and Tier 3 workstations, the pipeline introduces a Spatial Transformer Network (STN). The STN predicts a 6-parameter affine transformation matrix to unwarp plates skewed up to $45^\circ$, passing rectified tensors to ParseQ (Permutation Autoregressive Transformer), which leverages language priors to resolve ambiguous characters (such as distinguishing '0' from 'O' or '8' from 'B').Task 3: Facial Recognition & Alignment Under Night / Overhead SurveillanceFacial recognition at border posts requires processing camera feeds mounted at 4-meter heights (downward pitch angles $>35^\circ$), handling partial facial occlusions (e.g., face masks, caps, or scarves), and maintaining demographic parity across South Asian facial profiles.Operational TierFace Detection & Alignment EngineFeature Embedding BackboneCitation & OriginLicenseBenchmark Accuracy MetricsCombined Parameter ScaleTier 1 (Edge CPU)SCRFD-500MGhostFaceNetV2Deng et al., 2021 / Al-Fiky et al., 2023MIT99.1% LFW / 93.4% CPLFW~7.3M TotalTier 1 (Alternate)RetinaFace-Mob0.25AdaFace-MobileFaceNetDeng et al., 2020 / Kim et al., 2022MIT / Apache-2.098.6% LFW / 92.1% CPLFW~4.1M TotalTier 2 (Station GPU)SCRFD-2.5GAdaFace (IResNet50)Deng et al., 2021 / Kim et al., CVPR 2022Apache-2.096.2% IJB-C TAR (@1e-4 FAR)~46.1M TotalTier 3 (Command HQ)SCRFD-34GAdaFace (ResNet100) + SDD-FIQADeng et al., 2021 / Kim et al. / Terhorst et al.Apache-2.097.6% IJB-C TAR (@1e-4 FAR)~99.1M TotalAddressing Active-IR Grain and Downward PitchStandard face recognition loss functions, such as ArcFace or CosFace, enforce a fixed angular margin penalty ($m$) in the normalized embedding space during training.
